# Capture protocol — RUNG E limbs (b) and (c)

**One page. Under a minute. Operator-run.** Limbs (b) and (c) of RUNG E are
**felt observations about a named human** and are **OPERATOR-ONLY** — no agent
closes them, including the seats that built the rung (frame §2.3 :161-167; telos
:180-183). Limb (a) — two deliveries, each with a generation receipt, no human
assembly — is closed **mechanically** by the EX-4 receipt schema and attested
rite-disjointly by eunomia. This protocol does **not** touch limb (a).

## Before you write anything — name the ladder (ratified fence, §2.4)

> This capture is **RUNG-E (exec)**. It never writes into **RUNG-4 (team)**, and
> RUNG-4 is never graded in its place. If what you observed was a *teammate*
> (offers/account team) acting, that is RUNG-4 — a different record. An
> attestation that says "the telos is met" without naming the ladder is a
> **Gate-B refusal, not a pass.**

## Limb (b) — the exec named a figure back · *record when it happens*

Trigger: the CEO **or** the cofounder, **unprompted by this initiative**, names
back to you a **specific figure OR a specific named absence** from the readout.

Record, in **their own words**:

| field | what to write |
|---|---|
| date/time | when they said it |
| who | CEO / cofounder |
| the figure or absence | the exact number, or the exact "we have no X" they named |
| which readout occurrence | which dated delivery it came from — record the **`invocation_id`** of that delivery so this observation ties to the exact occurrence the EX-4 receipt schema keys on |
| their words (verbatim) | quote — a permalink to the Slack/message thread satisfies "durable and quotable" (DEFER-S-5 disposition). Record the permalink **against that `invocation_id`** so the felt (b)/(c) note joins back to the mechanical limb-(a) delivery receipt |

**Fails limb (b) — do NOT record as (b):** a generic acknowledgement with no
figure and no named absence ("thanks", "saw it", "looks good"). That is
receipt-of-email, not reading (**F-E2**).

## Limb (c) — the exec acted, and attributed it · *record when it happens*

Trigger: the exec **makes or changes a business decision they attribute to the
readout**. Record your **contemporaneous transcription of their own words**,
naming three things:

1. **the decision** — what they decided or changed;
2. **the figure or absence** it turned on;
3. **the direction of the change.**

**The decision need not be the one the readout pointed toward.** "Stop
investing" or "I don't trust that number" discharges (c) **identically** — that
is what *orientation, not steering* means at the predicate (R-16).

**Two guardrails, both fail (c) if crossed:**

- **F-E4 — attribution is the exec's speech act, never our inference.** If *you*
  connected their action to the readout and they did not say so, it does **not**
  discharge (c). Write "attributed by exec: yes/no." A `no` is not a limb-(c)
  close — record it as an observation, not a receipt.
- **F-E3 — steering voids the rung even when (c) is literally satisfied.** This
  is a property of the **readout's text**, not of the exec's act: if the readout
  recommended, ranked, or led with a call to action, then a steered reader
  acting is **not this rung**. You do not judge this at capture time — record the
  (c) observation, and flag it for the separate readout-text check (EX-1/EX-5/EX-6
  gate; PT-05 Q5.2 / PT-06 Q6.3).

## What this protocol does NOT do

- It does not close limb (a) (mechanical — EX-4 schema + eunomia).
- It does not grade the rung. A limb-(b) or limb-(c) note is **evidence for** an
  operator close, not the close itself.
- It records **RUNG-E only**. RUNG-4 (team acted-on, telos :155-162) is intact
  and unamended and is captured separately.
- It carries **no number of its own** and asserts **no decision** — recording the
  exec's words is not the same as recommending one (C-9, R-16).
