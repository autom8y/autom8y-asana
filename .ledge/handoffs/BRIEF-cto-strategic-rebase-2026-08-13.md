# One substrate, two stakeholders — the CTO frame

## The drift being corrected

The circulating summary says the near-term audience is *"your CEO and
cofounder, **not** the offers team."* That sentence conflates **sequencing**
with **priority**, and the record does not support the conflation:

- **R-6** ruled *exec first, team later — same eventual scope, different
  order*. Order, not replacement.
- **R-9** deferred the team-phase mission fork with a **named trigger the
  operator holds** ("the team phase begins") — a deferral chosen precisely so
  the fork would be ruled on *evidence* (demand, priced options) instead of on
  a surface PT-02 had measured as narrowed 32-to-1 by accident of scheduling.
- **R-15** kept the team's own definition of done — rung 4, *a teammate names a

  graded in its place. The team's bar was ratified *first* and survives
  untouched.

**The offers team was never demoted. Its prerequisites are what the current
wave is building.**

## The business picture, at CTO altitude

The offers board is where revenue operations actually happen: offers move
through sections, ad spend follows the board's state, and the reconciliation
service is the only instrument that checks the board against ad-platform
reality. Two audiences act on that instrument at two different altitudes:

- **Leadership (CEO/cofounder)** acts on *trust and allocation* — keep
  investing, believe the numbers, know what's broken. Their deliverable is
  orientation, and it is deliverable **now** because it needs no new data
  machinery (the one say-able readout rides existing verified data).
- **The offers team** acts on *the board itself, this week* — which offers are
  stuck, which corners went quiet, how long things sit in the launch pipeline.
  Their most actionable insights are **movement-class**, and those are blocked
  today **not by the exec pivot but by three data-integrity defects** this
  work found: history gaps silently filled with guesses indistinguishable from
  observations; a background warmer that has starved twelve of sixteen data
  types for two weeks; a query bug that reports newly-created offers as moved.

Sequencing exec-first was therefore not a value judgment between audiences. It
was: **serve the audience whose demand is proven and whose deliverable is
unblocked, while the same wave clears the blockers in front of the audience
whose demand is unmeasured and whose deliverable is data-gated.**
