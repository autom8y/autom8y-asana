## §3 KNC — THE ONE THREE-STATE CONVENTION (both planes)

> **R-12 is the risk this section exists to close:** *"Two three-state
> conventions land on one image event — the read plane and the loss plane each
> invent their own 'could not look'"* (shape `:1391`). One seam sprint, one
> convention. What follows is that convention. **Both planes instantiate it; a
> sprint that invents a second shape has breached this contract.**

### KNC-0 — The three states, named

Every state that can be "we could not look" has exactly **three** readings, and
they are never two and never four:

| role | meaning | how it is carried |
|---|---|---|
| **NAMED** | the question was asked and answered; the answer is known | a member of a CLOSED enum |
| **UNKNOWN** | the question could not be asked, or its answer could not be determined | the **literal enum member** `unknown` — a first-class member of the vocabulary, never a null, never a sentinel |
| **ABSENT** | there was nothing to ask about | the field is **omitted** / the record is **not written**. Positive selection (the estate's G-DENOM idiom) means it is correctly excluded from every count |

**`unknown` ≠ ABSENT, and this is the whole point.** A loss whose kind we cannot
name is still a LOSS and is still COUNTED (`unknown`). A booking that was never
lost has no record at all (ABSENT). Collapsing them is the defect this
initiative exists to cure, one level up.

### KNC-1 — DISCRIMINATOR form (MANDATORY for every state this contract mints)

A three-valued **CLOSED enum**. Never a boolean. Never a null. The discriminator
is always present on every emission of its line/row, so its ABSENCE is itself
meaningful (see CT-16).

### KNC-2 — NULLABLE-COMPANION form (INHERITED, and frozen to its existing sites)

A `T|null` field where `null` means **exactly** "could not be asked" and a value
(including `false`) means "asked and answered". Semantics inherited **verbatim**
from the in-repo precedent:

> `winner_is_collider` — **Three deliberately-distinct states:** `false` = a
> candidate WAS chosen and is not a collider (asked and answered) · `true` = a
> candidate was chosen and IS a collider · `null` = **no candidate was chosen, or
> provenance is unknown** (could not be asked).
> — `.sos/wip/CONTRACT-matcher-tier-tag-2026-09-03.md:271` (V-5) and `:354` (V-6),
> carried IDENTICALLY on both planes at rev 7.

**This contract mints NO new KNC-2 field.** The form is named so a builder can
recognize an existing one and never invert it. **A companion field may never use
`null` to mean "no".** That inversion is the exact collision R-12 names: on a
line carrying both `winner_is_collider` (where `null` = could-not-ask) and a
hypothetical `failed_leg_status: null` (where `null` would mean nothing-failed),
one reader's `null` is the other reader's opposite, at the plane, with no way to
tell. Refused by construction.

### KNC-3 — The ABSENT third state is OMISSION, never `null`, never a sentinel

Where a companion has nothing to describe, the field is **omitted from the line
and from the row**, and the **DISCRIMINATOR is what says so**. Two grounds, both
mechanical:

1. CloudWatch `$.field` selectors treat an absent field as a non-match; a `null`
   in that position is indistinguishable at the plane from KNC-2's
   could-not-be-asked. (SVR-8: the estate already reasons in exactly this
   register — the inverted-sentinel refusal at
   `ledger_level_observation.py:80-87`.)
2. A sentinel value ("none", `-1`, `""`) is a NAMED reading wearing an ABSENT
   costume. `NO_DEAD_LETTER_TTL_SENTINEL_HOURS` is the one legitimate exception
   in the estate and it exists precisely because a **gauge** must publish a
   number on every tick; a **discriminator** must not (SVR-8).

### KNC-4 — Reporting rule

Any zero reported off either plane carries its kind, or reads **"measured zero,
meter under repair"** (frame D-1/D-2; shape §7 Prescribed 5). A bare zero in any
successor artifact is rejected on those grounds.

## §4 F-M1 — RESOLVED: the mark is a **FIELD**, not a ninth outcome

### The question

The partial-read mark: a FIELD on the V-5 `name_evidence_outcome` line, a ninth
V-1 OUTCOME (`read_partial`), or both?

### Full option slate (enumerated before the recommendation)

| # | Option | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| (a) | **FIELD on the V-5 line (+ V-6 row per §6)** | V-1 stays CLOSED at eight; the completeness axis is orthogonal to the outcome axis and can cross it; INV-BASIS-shaped by construction | A rev-8 contract act: V-5 and V-6 gain fields, V-8 gains an invariant |
| (b) | **Ninth V-1 outcome `read_partial`** | One field to read; no schema growth | **Destroys the discrimination the predicate requires** (see below); breaks V-1's CLOSED rule at its second test; forces a false choice between naming the degradation and naming the decision |
| (c) | **Both** — field AND ninth outcome | Belt and braces | Two carriers for one fact, guaranteed to diverge; inherits (b)'s defect in full |
| (d) | Neither — carry it only on the retained ops line | No contract act at all | The ops line is **not countable** (V-4/CT-1: the counter does not reach CloudWatch; the V-5 line is the live surface). The predicate demands the mark be readable **AT THE PLANE without a trace join** — an ops-line-only mark is exactly the trace join it exists to retire |

### Why (a)

**1. V-1 is CLOSED at eight, and the head has already enforced that rule once.**
The `UnratifiedThresholdError` branch declined to mint a ninth label on exactly
this ground, in the head's own words:

> `★ THE UNRATIFIED CASE DELIBERATELY DOES NOT TOUCH THE COUNTER. CONTRACT V-1 is
> a CLOSED eight-value outcome vocabulary and an `unratified` label would be a
> ninth -- which V-1 says to SURFACE, not to add.`
> — `match_lead.py:135-140` (SVR-2)

> `★ THE UNRATIFIED CAUSE RETURNS ABOVE AND NEVER REACHES HERE. It has no V-1
> outcome by design (a ninth label is a seam defect to SURFACE, not to add)`
> — `match_lead.py:183-185` (SVR-3)

Minting `read_partial` would make this the second exception to a rule the head
enforced once at a real cost. A closed vocabulary that opens on the second ask
was never closed.

**2. The decisive argument: a ninth outcome is DESTRUCTIVE of the predicate's own
requirement.** The predicate requires *"the surviving leg's candidates were
**scored**"*. If the mark is the outcome, then the SCORING RESULT has nowhere to
go — `outcome` holds one value. A partial read that scores and BINDS must read
`outcome = matched` (the bind is real, a lead was attributed) **and** carry the
degradation. `outcome = read_partial` erases the bind; `outcome = matched` alone
erases the degradation. **No single-axis encoding can carry both**, and the
predicate demands both on one line.

**3. The contract already ratified this exact axis move.** V-2 made `tier` a
first-class field rather than a derived string-set over `outcome`, on three
reasons of which two transfer verbatim: *"As a string-set over `outcome` it would
silently widen the moment a ninth outcome appeared"* and *"W-COUNT's four classes
are a TIER axis crossed with an OUTCOME axis, **not one axis** (R-M7)"*
(`CONTRACT-matcher-tier-tag-2026-09-03.md:163-180`, SVR-4). Read-completeness is
a **third** axis over the same line. Crossing axes is the established pattern;
collapsing them is the established defect.

**4. INV-BASIS shape-compatibility requires a field.** `basis` is a REQUIRED
sub-object **on** the verdict, not a member of the verdict's outcome vocabulary
(`ADR-r3:195-199`, `:244-249`, SVR-13/SVR-14). A mark expressible as a basis-like
sub-object is a field or it is nothing (§12).

### The frozen mechanism — K-clauses

**K-1. The discriminator (KNC-1).** ONE new CLOSED enum, `read_completeness`,
with **exactly three** members:

| member | condition | 
|---|---|
| `complete` | every read leg answered |
| `partial` | at least one leg FAILED **and** at least one leg ANSWERED |
| `none` | every leg failed — the read did not happen |

**K-2. `unknown` is deliberately NOT a member of `read_completeness`, and the
reason is stated rather than absorbed.** The discriminator is computed inside the
loop that ran the legs (`activation_read_client.py:799-819`, SVR-5), which cannot
fail to know what it ran. A vocabulary member that nothing can ever legitimately
emit is a member only a defect can produce. **If a future call site cannot know
its own completeness, that is a seam defect to SURFACE, not an `unknown` to
emit.** This is a deliberate, reasoned exception to KNC-0's "UNKNOWN is a
first-class member of every vocabulary this contract mints" — it is the ONLY one,
and §8's loss vocabulary does carry `unknown` because its determination genuinely
can fail.

**K-3. The companions (KNC-3 — omitted, never null).**

| field | type | presence |
|---|---|---|
| `read_legs_failed` | int | **ALWAYS present.** `0` on `complete`, `n_legs` on `none`, strictly between on `partial` |
| `read_failed_leg` | str | present **iff** `read_completeness != "complete"`. The leg's name: `status_open` \| `status_null` (the two legs at `activation_read_client.py:177-182`, SVR-1). On `none`, the FIRST leg that failed |
| `read_failed_leg_status` | str | present **iff** `read_completeness != "complete"`. **THE STATUS CARRIED** — the `code` ClassVar of the raised `ActivationReadError` subclass, verbatim: `ACTIVATION_READ_UNAVAILABLE` \| `ACTIVATION_READ_CONTRACT_ERROR` \| `ACTIVATION_READ_REQUEST_ERROR` \| `ACTIVATION_READ_ERROR` \| the auth/scope subclasses' own codes (`activation_read_client.py:206-245`, SVR-6). **`unknown` when a non-`ActivationReadError` escaped the leg** — a partial must never be lost because the taxonomy missed |

**K-4. The seam split (which layer renders which state).** The read client
returns `complete` or `partial` and **never raises on a partial**; it raises
**only** when every leg failed, exactly as it does today. The stage's read-failure
handler renders `none` on that raise path. Consequence, stated: `read_completeness
= "none"` and `outcome = read_failed` are the SAME event seen on two layers, and
they must agree on every line (V-8 invariant, §6 and the rev-8 delta).

**K-5. The carrier on the read client.** `LeadCandidateSet` gains the mark as
fetch-level, narrowing-invariant state, beside `rows_before_dedupe` /
`pool_size` / `collided_keys` and on their stated precedent
(`activation_read_client.py:291-340`, SVR-7). `narrowed()` carries it through
**UNCHANGED** — a narrowing cannot make a partial read whole, and a
window-invariant field is the only kind that can be trusted downstream (the T-02
invariance argument, applied a third time).

**K-6. The ops sibling line — MINTED, because the mark landed as a field.** WS-A
names item (4): mint the partial sibling **only if** F-M1 lands the mark as a
field (frame `:889-892`). It did. Therefore:

- `name_evidence_read_failed` is **RETAINED UNCHANGED** (`match_lead.py:151-156`).
- A NEW ops line `name_evidence_read_partial` (WARNING) is minted on the
  human/ops layer, carrying `shape`, `read_failed_leg`, `read_failed_leg_status`,
  `error_type`, and the survivors' count.
- This is the V-5 "human layer / countable layer" pattern applied a third time
  (`CONTRACT:280-284`, `match_lead.py:202-211`, SVR-9). The countable layer stays
  ONE line.

**K-7. NO new metric label.** `NAME_EVIDENCE_MATCH` is labelled
`(shape, outcome)`. A partial mints **no** new label value: its outcome is
whatever the matcher decided (§5). V-4's low-cardinality contract is untouched,
and the transport truth (the counter does not reach CloudWatch — CT-1/SVR-1b of
the sibling contract) is unchanged.

**K-8. It is a rev-8 contract act, and the contract says so.** CONTRACT
`matcher-tier-tag` rev 7 is FROZEN. The V-5/V-6/V-8 changes above are authored as
a **rev-8 DELTA** at
`.sos/wip/CONTRACT-matcher-tier-tag-rev8-DELTA-2026-09-05.md`, applied at S-09 as

must emit), per the class-follows-the-edit rule.

## §5 F-M2 — RESOLVED: one leg failed, ZERO survivors reads `no_candidates` + `partial`

### The question

One leg failed and the surviving leg returned an empty set. Which kind?

### Full option slate

| # | Option | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| (a) | **`no_candidates` + `read_completeness = "partial"`** | The matcher RAN and DECIDED over the set it had; the mark carries the degradation on the orthogonal axis; the two-sidedness the predicate demands is expressible in three cells | Requires the reader to read two fields — which is the point of an axis |
| (b) | `read_failed` + the mark | One familiar bucket | **Poisons the outage denominator and breaks three-plane agreement** (below) |
| (c) | Its own V-1 name | Explicit | A ninth outcome — refused for §4's reasons, at their full strength |

### Why (a)

**1. The predicate fixes both poles, and (a) is the only reading that leaves them
fixed.** *"a genuinely empty pool with both legs OK still reads `no_candidates`,
and a both-legs-failed read still reads `read_failed`."* Under (a) the plane
reads three distinct cells with no trace join:

| case | `outcome` | `read_completeness` |
|---|---|---|
| genuinely empty, both legs OK | `no_candidates` | `complete` |
| one leg failed, zero survivors | `no_candidates` | `partial` |
| both legs failed | `read_failed` | `none` |

`stats count() by outcome, read_completeness` separates all three. That IS
predicate leg (a)'s *"distinguishable AT THE PLANE"*.

**2. (b) breaks three-plane agreement, mechanically.** `read_failed` is a
**stage-level degradation** (`CONTRACT:156`, SVR-10) and at the head it means the
fetch RAISED and no candidate set was ever obtained. The head says so in its own
words and draws the consequence:

> `- V-6 ROW: **NOT WRITTEN, and the line says so.** ``persisted=False`` is the
> honest value and it is not a formality: an attribution row records a matcher
> DECISION about a candidate set, and on this path no candidate set was ever
> fetched.`
> — `match_lead.py:174-178` (SVR-11)

On the one-leg-failed-zero-survivors path a candidate set **was** fetched — it
was empty, and the matcher decided over it. Routing it to `read_failed` would
either write no row for a read that produced a decision (losing the decision from
the row plane that `name_evidence_counts` aggregates) or write `persisted=True`
beside an absent row — *"the I-3 divergence V-8.3 exists to detect"*
(`match_lead.py:179-181`, SVR-11).

**3. (b) poisons the one denominator an operator most needs.** The
`read_failed` population is the upstream-outage rate. The head's PT04-C14 note
records that excluding it from the countable line *"had a denominator that
silently excluded exactly the upstream-outage population, which is the one an
operator most needs in it"* (`match_lead.py:163-166`, SVR-12). Adding a
scored-empty population INTO that rate is the same defect running the other way:
it inflates the outage rate with reads that did not outage. The head split
`_handle_read_failure` on precisely this class — *"Collapsing them onto one signal
would make a never-ratified constant indistinguishable from a flaky read
surface"* (`match_lead.py:130-133`, SVR-2).

**4. The mark is what stops `no_candidates` from being a lie.** At the head, a
partial-with-zero-survivors is **indistinguishable** from a genuine empty pool —
that is the defect. After DF-40 the outcome is honest ("the matcher found nothing
to bind") and the completeness field says the pool was not whole. Neither field
lies; together they are complete.

### The frozen mechanism

**M-1.** One leg failed, zero survivors ⟹ `outcome = no_candidates`,
`tier = none`, `read_completeness = partial`, `read_legs_failed = 1`,
`read_failed_leg` + `read_failed_leg_status` present. The V-6 row **IS** written
(a decision was made), `persisted = true`.

**M-2.** The generalisation, stated so no builder has to re-derive it: **the
outcome axis answers "what did the matcher decide over the set it had"; the
completeness axis answers "was the set whole". They are computed independently
and neither may be derived from the other.** Any outcome may cross any
completeness value except the pairs V-8 forbids (§6, N-3).

**M-3. F-P1's vacuity tell is converted to a checkable exit line, not decided.**
Frame `:1173` records that if F-P1 goes unnamed, option (c) — *"partial binds
normally with the mark as the only difference"* — happens **by default the moment
WS-A degrades to survivors**. This contract does not choose it. It refuses to let
it be silent: **S-04's PR body MUST state, in one sentence, which F-P1 option the
built head implements.** The mark makes every F-P1 option a one-line predicate
over `read_completeness`, so the operator's later word is a policy edit, never a
seam change.

## §6 F-M3 — RESOLVED: the mark rides the LINE **and** the ROW, with one stated exception

### The question

Line only, or line AND row?

### Why LINE AND ROW

**1. The in-repo precedent is exactly this pair, and it was ruled together.** V-6
carries `winner_is_collider` *"on T-02's own precedent (the dedupe pair was ruled
onto the line AND the row together) — which is what keeps CT-8's row-vs-log
two-sided check able to compare them"* (`CONTRACT:354`, SVR-15). A field on one
plane only cannot be cross-checked; the cross-check is the false-green detector.

**2. Without it, the persisted population cannot separate a partial-pool bind
from a whole-pool bind.** The row plane is what `scan_attribution_rows` /
`name_evidence_counts` aggregate. A W-COUNT class computed over rows that
silently mixes partial-pool and whole-pool binds is a denominator defect wearing
a count's name.

**3. Row == line is a checkable invariant** and therefore an instrument, not
bookkeeping (§6 N-3).

### The frozen mechanism

**N-1.** V-6 gains `read_completeness` (S) and `read_failed_leg_status` (S,
ABSENT when `complete`), carried **IDENTICALLY, not by reference**, so the row
schema is readable alone — the rev-7 convention for `winner_is_collider`
(`CONTRACT:354`), inherited.

**N-2. The exception, stated rather than discovered.** On
`read_completeness = "none"` **no V-6 row is written at all** — because
`outcome = read_failed` writes none today and must keep writing none
(`match_lead.py:174-181`). The mirror is therefore:
`read_completeness ∈ {complete, partial}` ⟹ the row carries it;
`read_completeness = "none"` ⟹ **no row**, and `persisted = false` on the line is
the agreement. V-8.3 is preserved exactly, not weakened.

**N-3. New invariants (V-8 additions, authored in the rev-8 delta).**

1. `read_legs_failed == 0` ⟺ `read_completeness == "complete"`.
2. `read_legs_failed == <legs attempted>` ⟺ `read_completeness == "none"`.
3. `read_completeness == "none"` ⟺ `outcome == "read_failed"` ⟺ `persisted == false`.
4. `read_completeness == "complete"` ⟹ `read_failed_leg` and
   `read_failed_leg_status` are **ABSENT** from both planes.
5. Where a V-6 row exists, `row.read_completeness == line.read_completeness` for
   the same `attribution_key`. A divergence is the I-3 false-green detector.

## §7 F-M5 — RESOLVED: a NEW kind-named LEVEL over a durable loss register, on its own event

### The question

The loss-count carrier: a kind **dimension** on `dead_letter_rows_current`,
**per-kind metrics**, or a **terminal-emission stream**?

### Full option slate

| # | Option | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| (a) | **Kind dimension on the EXISTING `ContenteBookingDeadLetterRowsCurrent`** | One metric, one filter | **BLINDS A LIVE SEV-1 PAGING ALARM.** See below — decisive and disqualifying |
| (b) | **Per-kind metrics on the existing event** | No dimension semantics to reason about | The existing event counts LIVE `dead_letter` rows only; a `ttl_reaped` loss has **no row left to count**. Cannot express the kind the predicate names third |
| (c) | **Terminal-emission stream only** (an edge at the loss instant) | Cheapest; survives the reap | **An edge self-clears.** It is precisely the defect `ledger_level_observation` was built to cure (`:5-17`). A resting-green alarm masks it by construction — the predicate forbids exactly this |
| (d) | **A NEW kind-named LEVEL over a durable loss register, on its own event, PLUS the existing terminal emission retained as the ops/edge layer** | Expresses all four kinds including `ttl_reaped`; leaves both live alarms untouched; a zero is PUBLISHED per kind per invocation, so a zero is a measured zero | Needs a durable register (§8/§9) and one additive terraform apply (§14) |

### Why (d), and why (a) is disqualified rather than merely worse

**(a) is disqualified on a mechanical platform fact.** CloudWatch:

> *"CloudWatch treats each unique combination of dimensions as a separate metric,
> even if the metrics have the same metric name. You can only retrieve statistics
> using combinations of dimensions that you specifically published."*
> — AWS CloudWatch concepts, §Dimension combinations (SVR-16)

> *"CloudWatch does not aggregate across dimensions for your custom metrics."*
> — ibid. (SVR-16)

The live alarm `ebi-contente-booking-dead-letter-level` watches
`ContenteBookingDeadLetterRowsCurrent` with **no `dimensions` block**
(`dead_letter_level_surface.tf:162-185`, SVR-17) and
`treat_missing_data = "missing"` (`:175`). Adding a kind dimension to that
metric's transformation therefore publishes to a **different metric**, the alarm's
datapoint stream stops, and — because missing data **retains state** — the alarm
**freezes at its last state forever**. That alarm's SEV-1 action is armed and
delivering: *"a firing here SENDS AN SMS"* (`:159`, SVR-18), and it is in ALARM
right now (frame X-3). Option (a) would freeze a live paging alarm on a real
outstanding loss. It is *"silent blindness dressed as health"* by the file's own
words (`:55-58`, SVR-19) — the exact failure class the file exists to remove.

The estate's own standing rule says the same thing one level up: *"Never blind the
old instrument to prove the new one"* (`ledger_level_observation.py:38-41`,
SVR-20).

**Why per-kind FIELDS on one event, and NOT a CloudWatch dimension even on the
new metric.** Three grounds, of which the third is decisive:

1. A metric filter accepts **at most three** dimensions: *"Enter the names and
   tokens for as many as three dimensions for the metric."* (SVR-21). Four kinds
   plus a total is five values.
2. *"If you assign dimensions to metrics that metric filters create, you cannot
   assign default values for those metrics."* (SVR-21) — this file's whole
   discipline is about `default_value` semantics (`:98-100`).
3. **DECISIVE: a dimension only publishes the kinds that APPEAR.** A kind with
   zero rows publishes no datapoint under its dimension value, so *"zero
   `receiver_refused` losses"* and *"the observer never ran"* are
   **indistinguishable at the plane**. That is a false green of exactly the class
   this initiative exists to kill. Per-kind **fields** on ONE event publish an
   explicit `0` for every kind on every invocation, so a zero is a **measured**
   zero and the ABSENCE of the event is the observer failing — and the two are
   discriminable, which is what makes I-5's resting green earnable.

### The frozen mechanism

**P-1. Nothing existing is touched.** `contente_booking_ledger_level`, its two
metric filters (`dead_letter_level_surface.tf:101-129`), both alarms
(`:162-185`, `:199-230`) and the pre-existing EDGE alarm remain **UNCHANGED and
ARMED**. Additive only.

## §8 THE LOSS VOCABULARY (FROZEN) — **two axes, not one**

### L-0 — A stated correction to the frame's enumeration

The frame names the WS-B loss-kind vocabulary as
`{receiver_refused, intake_fault, ttl_reaped, operator_resolved_lost}`
(`:919-923`). **Three of those four answer "why was the booking lost"; the fourth
answers "what did the operator do about it".** They are two axes, and this
contract separates them, with the reason stated rather than absorbed:

- A booking can be `receiver_refused` **and** resolved-as-lost by an operator
  word. On one axis those two fight for one slot and one of them is silently
  overwritten.
- `operator_resolved_lost` is the CARD's **RESOLVE-AS-LOST** operator word (frame
  X-1, `:1157`) — a disposition, applied later, by a person. A kind is stamped by
  the machine at the loss instant.
- The move has an in-contract precedent that was already ratified: *"W-COUNT's
  four classes are a TIER axis crossed with an OUTCOME axis, **not one axis**
  (R-M7)"* (`CONTRACT:178-180`, SVR-4).

**A builder must not collapse them back.** Surfaced at PT-01 as a correction, not
smuggled.

### L-1 — Loss KINDS (CLOSED at FOUR). *Why the booking was lost.*

Stamped ONCE, at the instant the loss became durable. **Never re-pointed** (the
`weak_evidence` discipline: *"RETIRED at the landing instant. Never re-pointed."*
— `CONTRACT:161`).

| kind | condition | receipt / where it arises |
|---|---|---|
| `receiver_refused` | the receiver answered non-2xx and the redrive ladder reached its cap | `reconcile_handler.py:509-559` — the `except` around the POST, at `attempts >= max_redrive` |
| `intake_fault` | our exception, validation failure, or stamp failure before or around the POST | the same handler for our-side exceptions; and the inbound 502 path (S-06's floor — mechanism is F-M4, **not decided here**) |
| `ttl_reaped` | a row evaporated **unresolved**, with no earlier loss event recorded | see L-1a — this is a real, currently-uncounted population |
| `unknown` | a loss is known to have happened but its kind could not be determined | **KNC-0's UNKNOWN.** A loss whose kind we cannot name is still a LOSS and is still COUNTED |

**Three-state, `unknown` ≠ none.** `unknown` is a witness that EXISTS with an
undeterminable kind. "No loss" is the **ABSENCE of a witness** (KNC-0 ABSENT) —
positive selection, never "absence of a stamp" (the estate's G-DENOM idiom,
`idempotency_ddb.py:678-680`, SVR-24).

**L-1a — what `ttl_reaped` actually names, and why it is not redundant.** Two
in-repo populations reach TTL without ever being counted:

1. **The stranded `redriving` row.** The `failed` sibling stamp discards its
   return, and a conditional miss there *"strands the row in `redriving` --
   outside `scan_redrive_candidates` (failed OR aged intent), so neither this
   sweep nor any later one ever selects it again"* (`reconcile_handler.py:560-566`,
   SVR-25). It is not `dead_letter`, so the LEVEL gauge never sees it. It sits
   until TTL and evaporates silently.
2. **The paused-sweep `failed` row.** *"`dry_run=True`, an unset `base_url`, or a
   REFUSE-ON-ABSENT-FUEL halt all stop the sweep from RECOVERING rows, and none of
   them stop dead-lettered rows from EXISTING. The moment you most need the level
   is the moment the recovery leg is paused."* (`ledger_level_observation.py:51-57`,
   SVR-26). A `failed` row under a paused sweep never reaches the cap, never
   becomes `dead_letter`, and reaches TTL uncounted.

**Precedence rule (the re-point refusal).** If a witness already exists for a row
when that row reaps, the reap does **NOT** change its kind. It sets the companion
`handle_reaped = true`, so the operator learns the recovery handle is gone
without the loss changing its name. `ttl_reaped` is used **only** when the reap
is the loss event — i.e. no witness existed.

### L-2 — DISPOSITIONS (CLOSED at THREE, plus ABSENT). *What was done about it.*

| disposition | meaning | arms a TTL? | in the LEVEL? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ABSENT** | **OUTSTANDING** — nobody has dispositioned this loss | no | **YES** — this is the level's positive-selection predicate |
| `resolved_landed` | a WS-C re-drive landed the booking; it is no longer a loss | **YES** — see §9 | no |
| `refused` | the re-drive was refused with a reason (WS-C `refused_with_reason`); the loss is real and permanent | **no** | no |
| `operator_resolved_lost` | the operator's RESOLVE-AS-LOST word; the loss is accepted | **no** | no |

**`held` is NOT a disposition.** See §10.

### L-3 — The loss-witness row (V-L, FROZEN)

One row per lost booking, on the **shared** `ebi-forwarding-idempotency` table
(zero schema mutation — the CI role lacks `dynamodb:UpdateTable`,
`idempotency_ddb.py:511-516`, SVR-27), under a **disjoint ns** and **disjoint
state attributes**, on the estate's own three-disjoint-state-machines idiom:
*"the three sweeps drive three disjoint state machines on the shared table"*

**L-3a — `stamp_applied` cures a named, live, two-instrument disagreement.** The
`dead_letter` stamp's return value is discarded, and when the conditional misses,
*"the metric, the summary counter and the CRITICAL log all announce a dead-letter

**The witness is written REGARDLESS of whether the stamp landed**, and

finally has a field that says why.

**L-3b — the witness is written UNCONDITIONALLY and IDEMPOTENTLY.** Conditional

**L-3c — THE WIRE VERB IS `UpdateItem`, NOT `PutItem` (recorded at rev 2; MTR-2).**

### L-4 — The kind-named LEVEL, and its positive selection

## §9 D-B1 — the by-construction floor, and its **BOUNDED** lifecycle

### The binding, restated

**ADDENDUM A-3 binds** (frame `:1327-1345`): the loss record must be
reaper-exempt **BY CONSTRUCTION**, never by a longer TTL. *"a longer fuse is still
a fuse"* (`CRIT-R3-delta-2026-09-04.md:323`, SVR-34). **A longer TTL is not a
floor and must not be authored.** Any proposal that lengthens the row TTL as its
floor meets D-B1 at the critic.

### The named construction

> **THE LOSS-WITNESS CONSTRUCTION.** The loss register is written on the estate's
> own **TTL-free conditional-put** pattern — `record_witness`, whose divergence
> from its TTL-bearing sibling is exactly one axis: *"the written ``Item`` NEVER
> carries a ``ttl`` key, at any value"* (`idempotency_ddb.py:238-243`, SVR-35) —
> and it **acquires a reap fuse only at the one disposition that provably ends its
> duty**, on the estate's own discharge pattern
> (`update_nudge_status(set_ttl_seconds=...)`, `idempotency_ddb.py:329-334`,
> SVR-36).

This is **FORK-3 option 1** (shape `:1541-1546`), taken with its recommendation's
own reason: it is the estate's own cure for the same conflation, its discriminator
is *"the literal presence/absence of the ``ttl`` attribute"* — mechanically
checkable — and **it has no moving part that can fail to run.** Option 2 (a
pre-reap sweep) is refused on the shape's own grounds: *"A sweep that must run. If
it does not run, the loss is silently uncounted — reintroducing the defect with an
extra moving part."* Option 4 is FORBIDDEN and is not authored.

### The bounded lifecycle — three bounds, each with a receipt (closes R-4)

R-4 states: *"A TTL-free record with no bounded lifecycle trades a silent loss for
a silently growing ledger ... A terminal record is not an immortal live row."*

**Bound 1 — ARITY (arithmetic).** One witness per lost booking, conditional-put,
idempotent (L-3b). The ceiling is the estate's own arrival rate, which the store
itself states: *"The live keyspace is bounded (~12-15 bookings/day x 7-day TTL)"*
(`idempotency_ddb.py:691-692`, SVR-37). At a hypothetical **100%** loss rate that
is ~5.5k payload-free typed records per year. The register is bounded by
**arrivals**, not by time.

**★ THAT FIGURE IS A CEILING, NOT A FORECAST (recorded at rev 2, correcting a
reading PT-01 caught).** ~5.5k/yr is what the register would hold at a
**hypothetical 100% loss rate** — every booking lost. It is an upper bound
computed to show the register cannot grow without limit; it is emphatically NOT an
expected volume, and no successor may cite it as one, size anything against it, or
read a number below it as evidence of health. The real rate is unmeasured; the
bound holds regardless of what it turns out to be, which is the whole point of
stating a ceiling rather than an estimate.

**Bound 2 — LEVEL ≠ LEDGER (the load-bearing one).** The **alarmed** number is
the OUTSTANDING count (`disposition` ABSENT, L-4), which is operator-drivable to
zero. Growth of the ledger does **not** grow the alarm, and the two are separately
readable. This is precisely what makes "a terminal record is not an immortal live
row" TRUE here: the witness stops being *live* the moment it is dispositioned,
and it stops being *counted* at the same instant, by the same predicate, with no
sweep and no deletion.

**Bound 3 — DISCHARGE.** At `disposition = resolved_landed` the witness arms
`ttl = now + WITNESS_DISCHARGED_TTL_SECONDS` (90 days —
`receipts/ddb_state_leg.py:80`, SVR-38) via the store's own conditional-update
fuse (`idempotency_ddb.py:353-359`, SVR-39). A recovered booking is **not** a loss
and must not persist as one. The other two dispositions deliberately do **not**
arm, on the estate's own precedent for exactly this population:

> `The terminal ``nudge_failed`` state deliberately does NOT arm a TTL: that
> population is the countable delivery-loss level and must persist.`
> — `idempotency_ddb.py:345-346` (SVR-40)

**Why a TTL re-arm and not a delete**, inherited verbatim so no builder proposes
one: *"an explicit delete would require widening a least-privilege role to grant a
destructive verb, in a service whose entire scar history is about rows
disappearing"* (`idempotency_ddb.py:336-344`, SVR-41).

## §10 WS-C terminal-set coherence — `{landed, refused_with_reason, held}` against the loss axes

WS-C's terminals live on the **RE-DRIVE** plane. The loss kinds live on the
**LOSS** plane. The mapping is total, and one cell is deliberately asymmetric.

| WS-C terminal | loss-witness effect | `loss_kind` | leaves the LEVEL? | arms a TTL? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `landed` | `disposition = resolved_landed`, `disposition_at` | **unchanged** (never re-pointed) | YES | **YES** — 90d (§9 Bound 3) |
| `refused_with_reason` | `disposition = refused`, `disposition_reason` = the status + reason code, **or `reason_unavailable`** (PC-4) | **unchanged** | YES | no — permanent of-record loss |
| `held` | `held_at` set; **`disposition` stays ABSENT** | unchanged | **NO** | no |

**The asymmetric cell, stated with its reason.** `held` is a terminal on the
re-drive plane (the act finished; the operator paused) and is deliberately **NOT**
a terminal on the loss plane. **A held loss is still an outstanding loss and the
alarm must keep saying so.** Treating `held` as a disposition would let an
operator silence a live loss by pausing it — the exact "resolved by silence"
failure the TTL-approach alarm exists to refuse (`dead_letter_level_surface.tf:201`,
SVR-42: *"This alarm exists so a durable loss is never resolved by silence."*).
`loss_rows_held` is published as its own field (§7 P-2) so a held population is
visible **inside** the outstanding total, never instead of it.

**Never re-`dead_letter`, never drop.** Every WS-C exit lands in exactly one of
the three rows above. There is no fourth path, and a re-`dead_letter` stamp after
a re-drive (the head's behaviour, CARD `:114-118`) is a contract breach, not a
degradation.

**`operator_resolved_lost` is the fourth exit and it is NOT a WS-C terminal** — it
is the operator's word taken **without** a re-drive (CARD RESOLVE-AS-LOST). It
sets `disposition = operator_resolved_lost` and behaves as `refused` on every
axis. Stated here so the four dispositions and the three terminals are visibly
reconciled rather than left to a builder.
