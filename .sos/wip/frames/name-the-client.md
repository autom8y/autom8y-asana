## 1. Mission — Realization Predicate

**End state: every active client's bookings arrive, are attributed to that client BY NAME on
the plane, and are countable — and no account activates without an end-to-end proof that its
pipe works.**

The mission has three joints and each one is currently broken somewhere in the record:
*arrive* (six offices have zero arrivals across the full 89-day retention — REPORT §3),
*named* (the plane emits an 8-hex GUID prefix while the account model keys on `office_phone`,
and **nobody holds the join** — C-15, RATIFICATION §3:44), and *countable* (a live booking
that never reaches its receiver is recorded under no kind — the parent's WS-B, carried).
The fourth clause — *no account activates without an end-to-end proof* — is C-17, and it is
the only clause that is preventive rather than diagnostic.

### The realization predicate — VERBATIM, carried into every sprint's exit criteria

> **"Verified-realized" = the operator's ratified receipt (C-7), carried VERBATIM into every
> sprint's exit criteria: a LIVE attributed booking naming that office, TWO-SIDED — a failure
> for the SAME office also names it, with its kind, never blank — held across the C-3
> denominator (ALL active clients, not one). NOT "PRs merged". DONE IS A BAR, NOT A DATE.**

Three properties of that predicate bind the whole envelope and must survive `/shape` intact:

1. **TWO-SIDED (C-7).** One live attributed booking is HALF the receipt. The other half is a
   *failure* for the same office that also names it, with its kind, never blank. A one-sided
   receipt is the exact defect the parent initiative exists to close, reappearing at client
   altitude. Checkable from the log plane, no human in the loop.
2. **DENOMINATOR = ALL ACTIVE CLIENTS (C-3).** Operator verbatim: *"3 sounds good, but for
   **all active clients—not just one**."* A green receipt for one office is not a partial
   pass; it is a **different claim**. The denominator is what makes this a client-class bar
   rather than an anecdote — and per §4 assumption 3 the meaning of "client" (contract state
   vs billing state) is **still unanswered**, which is why WS-DENOM exists.
3. **A BAR, NOT A DATE.** No calendar instant discharges this. The rung, the attester and the
   two-sided receipt discharge it. See the R-17 amendment in §2.
